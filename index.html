Everbit

An offline habit tracker. Tick off your day in one tap, fix any day you missed, and keep every streak on your phone. No account, no ads, no subscription.

What it does

  • One tap a day — tick a habit off, or count it up toward a daily target: pages, glasses, minutes.
  • Any schedule — every day, chosen weekdays, a number of times a week, or every few days.
  • Fix any day — forgot to log yesterday? Tap the day on the calendar and fill it in. Your streak is corrected.
  • Streaks and stats — current and best streak, times done, and your last 30 days, for every habit.
  • Reminders — a nudge at the time you pick, with a test button so you know they work.
  • Notes — add a line to any check-in.
  • Backup — export and import your whole history as one file.

Nothing is uploaded

Your habits live on your phone. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own, so your history has nowhere to go unless you export it yourself.

Pro

Free covers up to five habits with every feature above. Pro is one payment, never a subscription: unlimited habits, several reminders per habit, and spreadsheet export. It is tied to your Google account, so it comes back on a reinstall.

Privacy

The app collects nothing and uploads nothing. Read the full privacy policy.
