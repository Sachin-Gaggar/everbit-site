Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

The short version

Everbit does not collect, transmit or store your personal data. Your habits and their history stay on your phone, and the app has no server to send them to.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. None of it is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

  • Your habits. Their names, icons, colours, schedules and reminder times.
  • Your check-ins. Which days you did each habit, any number you logged, and any note you wrote.
  • Your settings. The theme you picked, and whether you have seen the welcome screen.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Backups and exports

You can export a backup file or a spreadsheet of your check-ins. The file is handed to Android's share sheet and goes only where you send it. What happens after that is governed by the privacy policy of whichever app or location you chose. Importing a backup reads only the file you pick.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by Android on your phone. The app asks for permission to show notifications only when you add a reminder, and sends nothing anywhere to deliver them.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We never see or receive your payment details. Google tells the app only whether the purchase exists, so it can unlock Pro. Your purchase is tied to your Google account, which is why it comes back automatically when you reinstall.

Accounts

There are none. The app has no sign-in and no user accounts.

Analytics and advertising

This version contains no analytics and no advertising.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If a future version starts collecting anything, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
