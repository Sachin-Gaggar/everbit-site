A little help.

Will my habits sync between phones?

Everbit has no developer-operated sync service. Move your habits yourself using JSON export and restore. Restore replaces the destination’s current habit data after validation and confirmation.

Can I change a missed day?

Yes. Choose a past date and correct the entry. You can record a value, add a note, skip, or clear an entry.

Why was my reminder late?

Your operating system controls notification delivery. Focus modes, notification settings, battery limits, and background restrictions can delay or silence reminders. Use the test notification in Settings to check permission.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings and choose Delete all local data. This removes habits, notes, preferences, reminders, and local recovery copies. Separately remove exported files or operating-system backups you want to erase. Store purchases remain attached to your store account.
