Privacy policy

Prepared 27 September 2026 for the native Android and iOS edition. This local release draft has not replaced the published policy.

Who operates Everbit

Everbit is developed by Sachin Gaggar, with support at poonamgaggarapps@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everbit stores the information you enter in the app’s private storage:

  • Habit names, symbols, kinds, goals, schedules, categories, cues, tiny fallback steps, reminder times, and pause/archive periods.
  • Dated check-ins, values, skipped days, and notes.
  • Appearance, skin, onboarding preferences, and local purchase-entitlement state.
  • Local recovery snapshots used to protect against failed writes or damaged data.

The app has no mandatory account, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated cloud service. We do not receive your habit history through the app.

Device backups and exports

Apple’s device or iCloud backup may include app data according to your system settings. Android’s automatic app backup is disabled in this edition. Operating-system services are governed by their providers’ terms and settings.

You can export a JSON backup or, with Pro, a CSV file. Files go to the app or location you choose through the system’s save or share interface. They are readable files, so anyone you share them with may read their contents. Import reads the file you select; a validated restore replaces the current habit data after confirmation.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally through Android or iOS. No remote push-notification service receives your habit names. Notifications can display a habit name on your lock screen; manage visibility and notification permission in your device settings.

Done records the corresponding check-in. Snooze schedules another local reminder. Operating-system restrictions may delay or prevent delivery.

Purchases

Optional Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase processed by Google Play or Apple’s App Store. The relevant store processes payment and provides purchase information used to verify access. We do not receive your card or bank details. The app keeps local entitlement state so paid features can remain available offline.

Purchase records remain with the store and are subject to its policies. Restore uses your account with the same store; an Apple purchase does not unlock Google Play, or vice versa.

Support and links

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. Use only the details needed to explain the issue. Opening this website or external links sends an ordinary web request to the hosting provider; this website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses no analytics or tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

Deleting an entry or restoring a backup removes or replaces current records. Older records may remain in device-local recovery snapshots until you use Delete all local data or remove the app. Delete all local data in Settings removes current habits, check-ins, notes, preferences, reminders, and known local recovery/legacy copies. It does not revoke your purchase or delete files already exported, system backups, emails, or store records. Those are managed through the corresponding service.

Children

Everbit is a general-purpose habit tracker and is not specifically directed at children. It has no developer account system and does not ask for a birth date.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when a release changes these practices. For questions or a request about information you sent to support, contact poonamgaggarapps@gmail.com.
